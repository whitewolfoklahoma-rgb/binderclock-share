      Help

      The short version of everything BinderClock does.

      How do I add my subs and vendors?

      On the Vendors tab, use the "Add a vendor" form — name and trade are enough to start. If your list already lives in a spreadsheet, save it as a CSV with the columns name, trade, contact, email, agent, agent_email and use Import roster (CSV); vendors already on the list are skipped, never duplicated.

      How does a certificate get checked?

      On the Cert Intake tab, pick the vendor and enter what the certificate says (in the live product the fields fill from the uploaded document). The moment you save, BinderClock scores the cert against the requirements on the Vendors tab — limits, additional insured, waiver of subrogation, holder name, and every expiration date — and shows you the checklist in plain English.

      What do the statuses mean?

      Compliant — everything required is on file and current. Expiring — compliant today, but a policy ends within 30 days. Expired — at least one required policy has lapsed. Deficient — paperwork is on file but something is short: a limit below your minimum, a missing endorsement, a wrong holder name. Missing — no certificate on file at all.

      How does the chasing work?

      The Chase Queue lists everyone who owes you paperwork, worst first, with the email already written — addressed to the insurance agent (who can issue the cert) with the sub copied, and a no-login upload link so nobody needs an account. The ladder runs at 30, 14, 7, and 2 days before an expiration, and daily once a policy has lapsed. Mark a chase done and the queue re-sorts itself.

      What is the audit packet?

      One dated file — CSV or print — listing every vendor, every policy line, every expiration, each vendor's status, and every open deficiency. It is the document an owner, GC, or insurance auditor asks for at renewal or after an incident. Export it from the Dashboard any time.

      Where does my data live? How do I back it up?

      In this demo edition, everything lives on this device, in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded. Export the audit packet CSV to keep a dated copy, and use Reset below to restore the fictional sample data or Clear to empty the app. Clearing your browser's site data erases the roster, so export before you do.
